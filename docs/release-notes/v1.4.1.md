## New in 1.4.1

- Restore the song title fade when changing tracks in the current player.
- Start the next song when skipping from a paused notification player.
- Refresh playback icons on Android and Android TV, including curved repeat arrows and a distinct repeat-one state.
- Arrange expanded notification controls as repeat, previous, play/pause, next, and favorite where supported by the device's system UI.
- Preload nearby cover artwork and reuse prepared images to reduce artwork loading interruptions in media notifications.
- Set or cancel a TV sleep timer from the connected phone. Active timers follow playback handover with their remaining time, including end-of-song timers, and continue on the TV after the phone disconnects. Update both devices to use this feature.
- Balance the player footer buttons, reduce extra spacing in the options menu, and move Share directly below Copy link.
- Keep the About option accessible above the mini player in Settings.
