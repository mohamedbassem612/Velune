## Smarter Quick Picks

- Get recommendations shaped by how much you listen, your recent and long-term taste, and the songs you repeatedly skip.
- Discover music related to several songs you enjoy, alongside YouTube Music chart tracks that match your taste.
- Your listening habits gain priority over old likes as you use the app.
- Enjoy more variety with fewer repeated artists and duplicate uploads. The discovery list leaves out your liked songs and the last 30 distinct songs you played.
- See fresh picks in the same session, with a local fallback when online recommendations are unavailable.

Update over your existing installation to keep your listening history and preferences. Before uninstalling or moving to another device, use Backup and Restore to preserve them.
